About M. Gyamprah Enterprise

A growing Ghanaian enterprise built on reliability, sourcing power and long-term partnerships.

Our story

Supplying the materials that build Ghana.

M. Gyamprah Enterprise (MGE) started as a local supplier of building materials to small-scale contractors. Today, we have grown into a multi-division industrial business, supplying cement, iron rods and roofing materials, importing galvanized and pre-painted steel coils from China and Turkey, and operating a modern fuel station serving motorists, fleets and bulk buyers.

Our growth has been driven by one principle: be the supplier our customers can rely on. Contractors need stock on site when they need it. Developers need pricing they can plan a budget around. Fleet operators need fuel that's clean, reliable and on time. We deliver on all three.

From our base in Ghana, we combine local market knowledge with international sourcing, keeping costs competitive and quality consistent across every order.

What we stand for

Our core values

The principles that guide every delivery, every quote and every partnership.

Commitment to Quality

Every cement bag, every steel coil, every litre of fuel meets the standards our customers expect. Mill certificates, supplier audits and on-site checks are part of how we work.

Reliable Sourcing

Direct relationships with mills in China and Turkey, plus trusted local suppliers, give us the supply security to keep your project moving, even when markets are tight.

Customer Satisfaction

From a single bag of cement to a 30-tonne steel container, every customer gets the same priority. We measure ourselves on repeat business, not one-off sales.

Contractor Partnerships

Dedicated account management for contractors with running supply needs. Pre-agreed pricing, scheduled deliveries, and credit terms for trusted partners.

Efficient Delivery

A streamlined logistics network across Ghana means quotes are turned around in hours, not days, and deliveries arrive on the day they were promised.

Long-Term Relationships

We are not in the business of one-off transactions. Our biggest customers have been with us for years, and we work to keep it that way.
